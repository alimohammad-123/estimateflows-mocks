How it works

Four places painting leads get lost, and what we do instead

A homeowner moves from ad to inquiry to booked estimate to the estimator at the door. Most contractors lose them at one of these four steps.

Step 1 · Getting inquiries

Service-area demand capture

Meta ads for the project types and neighbourhoods you want, plus your own past leads.

  • Most doRun broad "painters near me" ads or buy shared leads, and leave the old lead list sitting in a spreadsheet.
  • The problemInquiries come from outside your area, for work you don't want, and the same lead goes to three other painters.
  • What it costsEstimator hours driving across town for small jobs you lose on price. Past customers who would hire you again call someone else.
  • What we doBuild Meta ads around your profitable project types and the neighbourhoods you choose, and reactivate your own past leads and customers.
  • So you canFill the estimate calendar with the work you want, where you want it.

Step 2 · Answering

Instant inquiry response

A fast reply to new calls and forms, missed-call text-back included.

  • Most doCall back when the crew packs up for the day, or the next morning.
  • The problemHomeowners contact two or three painters and talk to whoever gets back to them first.
  • What it costsYou pay for the inquiry and lose it before you ever speak.
  • What we doSend a fast reply to new calls and form inquiries, with missed-call text-back, evenings and weekends included.
  • So you canStay on the job and still be one of the first painters they hear from.

Step 3 · Checking the job

Job qualification and routing

Scope, address, timing and fit, before anyone drives out.

  • Most doPut whoever calls straight onto the estimator's calendar.
  • The problemThe estimator finds out at the door that it's one bathroom 40 minutes away, or a homeowner who's only collecting prices.
  • What it costsHalf a day per wasted estimate, and fewer slots for the jobs you'd win.
  • What we doCollect the scope, address, timing and fit before your estimator spends any time on it.
  • So you canSend your estimator only to homes that match your service area and project types.

Step 4 · Getting them there

Confirmation and recovery

Confirmations, reminders and follow-up on no-shows and unbooked homeowners.

  • Most doBook it and hope they're home. No-shows get written off. Homeowners who didn't book get forgotten.
  • The problemHomeowners forget, need to reschedule, or go quiet with a question nobody answered.
  • What it costsEmpty slots on the calendar, and inquiries you already paid for going cold.
  • What we doConfirm and remind booked appointments, follow up no-shows, and follow up homeowners who didn't book.
  • So you canKeep a calendar of estimates that actually happen.

The Done-for-You guarantee

What we guarantee, word for word

Estimate appointments, not signed or completed painting jobs. Read the conditions before the review call so there are no surprises.

Guarantee

15 qualified attended estimate appointments in the first 30 days. Appointments generated through database reactivation count toward the same 15 if they meet the same qualification, attendance, and CRM-recording criteria. If EstimateFlows misses the target, it will continue work at no additional service fee for another 30 days; if the same 15-appointment target is still unmet at the end of that extension, refund all program fees and reimburse all client ad spend. This is not a guarantee of signed or completed painting jobs.

Conditions

Done-for-You only. Available only if you provide your complete historical lead list before campaign launch. Your side: at least $2,000 per month of ad spend paid to the advertising platform; the complete historical lead list before launch; at least 10 calls per week to eligible prior painting customers; reasonably open estimate capacity; keep your service areas and advertised work available; run and reschedule estimates in good faith and follow up on quotes; keep the approved response and booking operations active; give reasonable access, approvals, assets and timely creative review; capture before, mid-job and after photos or video for each job. Full terms are in your written agreement.

What counts

A qualified attended estimate appointment means: the project matches the agreed service area, project type and homeowner intent; the homeowner (or decision-maker) and your estimator attend and the estimate is conducted. Bookings, cancellations and no-shows don't count.

Fit

Who this is for, and who it isn't

A good fit

  • You own an established residential painting company in the US.
  • Your crews can take on more work, and your estimator has open time.
  • You have a list of past leads and customers, in a CRM or a spreadsheet.
  • You can put money into ads each month, paid to Meta directly.

Not a fit

  • You're booked out and can't run more estimates in the next 30 days.
  • You want to buy shared leads by the piece.
  • You want someone to guarantee your close rate. Our guarantee covers estimate appointments only.
  • Nobody on your team will follow up on quotes after the estimate.
