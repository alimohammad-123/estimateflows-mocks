How it works · 4 parts

Four places a lead leaks, in the order it moves

Each part fixes one stage between a homeowner seeing your ad and your estimator walking up the driveway.

Stage 1 · Getting found

Service-Area Demand Capture

Most of the market

Buys shared leads or runs generic "painters near me" ads. Past leads and customers sit untouched in the CRM.

Why that's a problem

Shared leads go to several companies at once. Generic ads pull small jobs from towns you don't want to drive to.

What it costs you

Estimator hours on price shoppers and drive time, while people who already know you hear nothing.

So instead we

Build Meta ads around your profitable project types and neighborhoods, and reactivate your own database of past leads and customers.

So you can

Fill the calendar with the work and the areas you actually want.

Most of the market

Calls back after getting off the ladder, often hours later. Evening and weekend inquiries wait until Monday.

Why that's a problem

Homeowners send the same form to several painters and talk to whoever answers first.

What it costs you

Your numbers say – inquiries a month wait more than 5 minutes. That's – of job value at your own close rate.

So instead we

Give new calls and form inquiries a fast reply, including evenings and weekends. Missed calls get a text back.

So you can

Stay on the job and still be the company that answered.

Stage 3 · Before the estimate

Job Qualification & Routing

Most of the market

Books every estimate that asks, then learns on the driveway that it's one bedroom, 40 miles out, next year.

Why that's a problem

Your estimator's time is the bottleneck. Every bad estimate pushes a good one back.

What it costs you

Lost afternoons, fuel and quotes for work you never wanted.

So instead we

Collect scope, address, timing and fit before your estimator spends any time.

So you can

Send your estimator only to estimates worth running.

Stage 4 · Showing up

Confirmation & Recovery

Most of the market

Books the appointment and hopes the homeowner is home. Homeowners who didn't book never hear from you again.

Why that's a problem

No-shows and forgotten inquiries look like a lead problem when they're a follow-up problem.

What it costs you

Empty slots you held open and homeowners who went with someone else by default.

So instead we

Confirm and remind every appointment, follow up no-shows, and follow up homeowners who didn't book.

So you can

Pull up to homes where someone is expecting you.

The guarantee · DFY only

The whole guarantee, word for word

15 qualified attended estimate appointments in the first 30 days. Appointments generated through database reactivation count toward the same 15 if they meet the same qualification, attendance, and CRM-recording criteria. If EstimateFlows misses the target, it will continue work at no additional service fee for another 30 days; if the same 15-appointment target is still unmet at the end of that extension, refund all program fees and reimburse all client ad spend. This is not a guarantee of signed or completed painting jobs.

Done-for-You only. Available only if you provide your complete historical lead list before campaign launch. Your side: at least $2,000 per month of ad spend paid to the advertising platform; the complete historical lead list before launch; at least 10 calls per week to eligible prior painting customers; reasonably open estimate capacity; keep your service areas and advertised work available; run and reschedule estimates in good faith and follow up on quotes; keep the approved response and booking operations active; give reasonable access, approvals, assets and timely creative review; capture before, mid-job and after photos or video for each job. Full terms are in your written agreement.

A qualified attended estimate appointment means: the project matches the agreed service area, project type and homeowner intent; the homeowner (or decision-maker) and your estimator attend and the estimate is conducted. Bookings, cancellations and no-shows don't count.

Fit

Who this is for, and who it isn't

A good fit if you

  • Own an established residential painting company in the US
  • Have room on the calendar for more estimates in the next 30 days
  • Run your estimates and follow up on quotes
  • Have past leads and customers on file, ideally the complete list
  • Can pay for ads directly to the platform each month

Not a fit if you

  • Want a promise of signed contracts. We don't make one.
  • Can't take more estimates right now
  • Want shared leads to work through on your own
  • Won't run ads paid to the platform
  • Mostly bid commercial or new construction work

FAQ

Straight answers

Is this a guarantee of jobs?

No. The Done-for-You guarantee covers 15 qualified attended estimate appointments in the first 30 days. This is not a guarantee of signed or completed painting jobs. You run the estimates and close the work.

What do I need to provide?

For Done-for-You with the guarantee:

  • At least $2,000 per month of ad spend paid to the advertising platform
  • The complete historical lead list before launch
  • At least 10 calls per week to eligible prior painting customers
  • Reasonably open estimate capacity
  • Your service areas and advertised work kept available
  • Estimates run and rescheduled in good faith, and quotes followed up
  • The approved response and booking operations kept active
  • Reasonable access, approvals, assets and timely creative review
  • Before, mid-job and after photos or video for each job
Do I need to spend on ads?

Yes. Ad spend is paid to the platform directly, not to us. The Done-for-You guarantee needs at least $2,000 per month of ad spend paid to the advertising platform. For Done-with-You we set the level with you on the call.

What happens on the review call?

It's a 30-minute Painting Pipeline Review on video, with no obligation. We go through your numbers (the same ones in the worksheet above), your crews, service area and past lead list. You leave knowing which program fits, whether the guarantee applies to you, and what you'd need to provide.

What counts as a qualified attended estimate appointment?

A qualified attended estimate appointment means: the project matches the agreed service area, project type and homeowner intent; the homeowner (or decision-maker) and your estimator attend and the estimate is conducted. Bookings, cancellations and no-shows don't count.

Where are the prices?

Program fees depend on which program fits, so we cover them on the review call.
